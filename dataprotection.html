Privacy Policy


This website uses self-hosted Matomo for optional visitor analytics, only with your consent. Website and analytics data are hosted in Europe and are not transferred to the USA. We do not use Google Analytics or advertising trackers.


Optional local analytics


With your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), Matomo at matomo.ivancevicart.com measures page visits and link interactions, using browser information and first-party cookies. Rejecting optional cookies prevents the analytics script from loading and prevents analytics requests. Use Cookie settings in the footer to withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal stops future tracking and removes accessible Matomo cookies without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.


Cookies and browser storage


Necessary localStorage: ivancevic-cookie-consent records your choice, timestamp and notice version for 180 days; site-lang stores your chosen language until you clear browser storage. Optional Matomo cookies: _pk_id.* (visitor identifier, 180 days), _pk_ses.* (visit session, 30 minutes), and _pk_ref.* (referral source, 180 days, if set). These analytics cookies are enabled only after consent. No marketing cookies are used. Cookie settings let you accept, reject or change your choice at any time. Clearing browser storage or expiry prompts a new choice. Cookie lifetimes do not define how long analytics records are retained on the server.


Contact requests


If you contact us by form, phone or email, we process the details you provide to respond to your request and, where applicable, take steps before entering a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Form messages are stored locally and sent to our configured email recipient. Service providers may process these details to deliver hosting and email services.


Technical operation


Technical access data may be processed to deliver and secure the website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Fonts and icons are provided without automatic requests to Google or external font and icon CDNs. External links connect to their respective providers only when you follow them.


Your rights


Under the GDPR, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction and, where applicable, portability of your personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent. You may lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (dsb.gv.at).
